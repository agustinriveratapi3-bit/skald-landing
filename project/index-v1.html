CH.05 / WHAT WE OBSESS OVER
F.01 · Pacing

Pace is everything.

The cut that lands isn’t the one that’s correct — it’s the one that breathes. We treat runtime like a score: silence, rest, beat.

Δt ≈ story Rhythm via subtraction
F.02 · Color

Color is character.

Grade isn’t a finishing pass. It’s a casting decision. We build LUTs from references that don’t exist yet, then sit with them in the dark until they do.

ci = f(scene, intent, lens) Color as a function of meaning
F.03 · Sound

Sound carries story.

The mix is the second edit. Foley, room tone, the absence of music — the things audiences never name but always feel.

Sdb ≈ truth Mix as a structural element
F.04 · Decisions

Now 37.9% more decided.

We recalibrated our cut‑rate after watching too many edits hedge. Every frame should mean something or get cut. There is no third option.

D = 1 − hedge2 Decision quotient
